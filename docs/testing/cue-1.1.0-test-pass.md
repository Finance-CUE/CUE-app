CUE 1.1.0 test pass

Install (Android only): open the APK link we send you on your phone, tap Download, open the file, and allow "Install from this source" when Android asks. Install it over the old CUE: your account and data stay.

Report each problem as: step number, what you did, what you saw, screenshot if possible.
You can use made-up amounts. Nothing here moves real money; CUE only records what you type in.

Already known, no need to report:
- Nudge notifications start with "[PLACEHOLDER]". The wording is being written by our psychologist.
- In Khata, two different people with the same name are combined into one.
- Google sign-in works only for Gmail addresses we have added as testers. If it says access is blocked, send us your Gmail address.
- There is no iPhone version yet.
- The very first open after a long quiet period can take a few seconds to load.

Test the steps below one by one and write your result next to each (WORKS / DOESN'T / what happened).

Sign up, sign in and recovery
1. Open CUE, tap Create account, and fill in full name, email, a 10-digit Indian mobile number and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number). A screen asks for a 6-digit code. The email (from no-reply@cuemoney.in) arrives within a minute; check spam if not. Enter the code: you land on "Before we begin".
2. On the code screen, tap Resend: a new code arrives. An old code no longer works.
3. Try creating another account with the same email, then with the same mobile number: each is refused with a clear message.
4. Log out, then tap Continue with Google and pick your Gmail. If it's a new account you're asked for your mobile number once, then you're in. No code is needed.
5. On the login screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email, enter the code from the email and a new password: you're signed in, and the old password no longer works.

Assessment and your money profile
6. Tap Start the assessment and answer about 8 questions. Fully close the app and reopen it: you come back where you left off.
7. Finish all 18 questions and the "money why" question. Your money profile shows your primary pattern with Strengths, Blind spots, What drives you, What you need and Where you can grow, then two more patterns under "Also in your mix", then your six dimensions with a bar each.
8. Read your profile: does it feel like you? Tell us honestly (spot on / partly / not at all) and why.
9. Tap Enter CUE, enter a bank balance (cash is optional) and tap Continue. You land on Today.
10. On the You tab, the card shows your pattern with the other two "in the mix". Tap See your full profile: the same profile from step 7 opens.

Today
11. The greeting uses your first name, and Net worth matches what you entered.
12. Tap Cash and type "Chai", "Auto to office", "Rent" or "DMart": the category picks itself (Food, Transport, Bills, Groceries). Tap Log: the spend appears under Today and today's bar grows.
13. Log a spend and choose Yesterday, and another with Another day and a date last week (DD/MM/YYYY). A future date is refused.
14. Tap the ‹ arrow next to the week title: last week shows, with the spend from step 13. Tap a day to see its spends. The › arrow brings you back to this week.
15. Tap a spend, then Edit: change its name, amount, category or date and Save. The list, totals and bars update. Subscription payments have no Edit button; that's expected.
16. Tap a spend and Delete it: it disappears and the totals shrink.
17. Pull down on Today to refresh: everything reloads and nothing duplicates.

Balance and net worth
18. After logging spends, Net worth on Today has gone down by those amounts and says how much was spent since you last updated your balance.
19. Tap Update balance: Bank already shows the amount after those spends. Change bank or cash and Save: Net worth matches exactly what you typed, and the "spent since" line is gone.
20. On the You tab, tap the small chart: Net worth opens. On your first day it shows a line starting today. Over the next days the line moves with your spends and balance updates.

Khata (lend / borrow)
21. Tap Lend / Borrow, choose I lent, enter a name, amount and note, and Save. It appears under Money lent in Khata.
22. Add an I borrowed entry for someone else: it appears under Money borrowed.
23. Tap Pick from contacts: Android asks for contacts permission once. Saying Deny doesn't crash anything; you can still type the name.
24. Adding a Khata entry does not change Net worth: it is only a record.
25. Lend more than your current balance: CUE asks "This seems to be more than your current balance". Edit amount takes you back; Save anyway saves it.
26. Open someone who owes you and tap Mark received: the entry leaves the list and your Net worth goes up by that amount.
27. Open someone you owe and tap Mark paid back: CUE asks Cash or UPI. Pick one: Net worth goes down by that amount and Today shows "Paid back to [name] via UPI" (or "in cash").

Subscriptions
28. Tap Subscription and add "Netflix", ₹199, billing day = tomorrow's date: it shows under Upcoming as "Due tomorrow".
29. Tomorrow at about 10 am, a notification asks "Is Netflix still active?". Tap it: CUE opens and asks the same question. Tap Yes: the payment is logged as a spend for that day.
30. For another subscription, answer No: it moves to Stopped and isn't asked about again.

Nudges (these need a few days of normal use)
31. Allow notifications when Android asks. If you deny them, the You tab says notifications are off and Open settings takes you to turn them on.
32. The three Nudge preferences switches stay the way you set them after closing and reopening the app.
33. Log your real spends for 3 or more days. Tell us whether nudge timing and tone felt helpful or annoying. At most one spending nudge per day, and none late at night.
34. On Sunday evening (around 6 pm) you get a weekly reflection. Turn Weekly reflection off: it stops.

Account and general
35. Close and reopen the app over a few days: you stay signed in.
36. Sign in on a second phone with the same account: both stay signed in. Signing out on one doesn't sign out the other.
37. Sign out: you're back on the login screen and get no more nudges. Sign back in: all your data is still there.
38. If two of you share a phone: sign out, have the other person sign in to their own account, and check none of the first person's spends, Khata, balance or profile show up.
39. Airplane mode, then open each tab: you see a clear message, not a crash. Turn it off and pull down: data comes back.
40. Anything confusing, ugly, slow or missing: write it down. First impressions matter most.
