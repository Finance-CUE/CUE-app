This build adds Goal Jars: set money aside for goals like a trip or a laptop, inside your own accounts, and watch each jar fill up.

Install (Android only): open the APK link on your phone, tap Download, open the file, and allow "Install from this source" if Android asks. Install it over your current CUE: your account and data stay.

Report each problem as: step number, what you did, what you saw, a screenshot or screen recording if possible.
Amounts can be made up. CUE only records what you type; no real money moves.

How jars work, so you're not surprised:
- A jar does not move money out of your bank. It marks part of an account's balance for a goal.
- Adding to or taking from a jar never changes your bank balance or your net worth.
- "Free" on an account means its balance minus what its jars hold.
- Automatic contributions always ask first. Nothing is added without your tap.

Already known, no need to report:
- Jar icons are emoji; photos are not supported.
- "Every payday" needs your salary set up (Income screen › Set up salary).
- Everything listed as known in the 1.2.1 guide still applies.

Test the steps below one by one and write your result next to each (WORKS / DOESN'T / what happened).

On Today
1. Between "Where your money moves" and Coming up there is MY GOAL JARS. With no jars it says "Every big goal starts somewhere. Create your first jar." with Create jar.

Create a jar
2. Tap Create jar. It walks through 8 steps, one per screen, with a progress bar: Name, Target, Target date, Icon, First contribution, How you add, Account, Create.
3. Try Next with an empty name or ₹0 target: a short message appears under the field.
4. Target date: try "No target date", then go back and enter a date after today.
5. Pick an icon, enter ₹10,000 as the first contribution, choose "When I choose", pick your bank, and tap Create jar.
6. The jar appears in the carousel: icon, name, "₹10,000 / ₹30,000", a progress bar, 33% and "by [date]".
7. Above the cards: "₹10,000 set aside across 1 jar". At the end of the row: "+ Create new jar".
8. Your net worth and bank balance are the same as before creating the jar.
9. Under Your accounts, the bank row now says "₹10,000 in jars · ₹… free".

The jar screen
10. Tap the jar: target, in the jar, remaining, progress, target date, suggested each month, the account, and Contribution history.
11. Add money ₹2,000. A message says "Added ₹2,000 to … · Undo". Tap Undo: it goes back.
12. Try to add more than the account has free: CUE refuses and says how much is free.
13. Withdraw ₹4,000. The jar goes down; your bank balance does not change. Try to withdraw more than the jar holds: CUE refuses.
14. Edit goal: change the name, icon and target. Move the jar to another account: its money moves with it (check both account rows).
15. Pause contributions: the bar turns grey and the card says "Paused". Resume brings it back.
16. Add money until the jar reaches its target: "Reached 🎉" shows once, the bar is full, and the card says "Reached 🎉". Closing the celebration doesn't show it again.
17. Archive a jar: CUE says the money goes back to the account's free balance. It leaves the carousel and the account's "in jars" amount drops.

Suggested contributions
18. Create a jar with How you add = Monthly and ₹2,000 each time. The jar screen says "Monthly · ₹2,000 · next [date] · asks before adding". Nothing is added on its own.
19. When a contribution is due, the card shows "Add money?" and the jar screen asks "Add ₹2,000 to …?" with Approve and Skip this one. Approve adds it; Skip adds nothing. Both move the next date on.
20. "Every payday" without a salary set up says to set up salary first.

On track
21. A jar with a target date that is well behind schedule shows "A little behind". A jar with no target date never does.
22. A jar whose date has passed without reaching the target shows "Target date passed", and tapping that line on the jar screen opens Edit goal.

Safety checks
23. Spend from the jar's account until it has less than the jars hold. The jar screen says "Your jars hold more than [account] has right now". The jar amount doesn't shrink on its own.
24. Try to archive an account that a jar uses: CUE asks you to move the jar or archive it first.
25. With a salary set up, the "free to spend today" line goes down when you put money into a jar on a bank or cash account.

Quick Add and the rest
26. Tap +, then Goal Jar. Pick a jar (or "+ New jar"), enter an amount and add it.
27. View all on MY GOAL JARS opens every jar in a grid with Create jar.
28. Tap the eye icon: jar amounts show ₹ •••••; percentages stay visible.
29. Airplane mode, then reopen: MY GOAL JARS says "Couldn't load your jars." with Try again, on its own.
