CUE 1.0.0 (build 1) test pass

Install (Android only): open this link on your phone, tap Download, open the file, and allow "Install from this source" when Android asks.

Report each problem as: step number, what you did, what you saw, screenshot if possible.
You can use made-up amounts. Nothing here moves real money; CUE only records what you type in.

Already known, no need to report:
- The app icon and splash are placeholders until the final logo is ready.
- Nudge notifications start with "[PLACEHOLDER]". The wording is being written by our psychologist.
- Your email address is not verified at signup.
- Spends can be deleted but not edited.
- In Khata, two different people with the same name are combined into one.
- There is no iPhone version yet.
- The very first open after a long quiet period can take a few seconds to load.

Test the steps below one by one and write your result next to each (WORKS / DOESN'T / what happened).

Sign up and assessment
1. Open CUE, tap Create account, and fill in full name, email, a 10-digit Indian mobile number and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number). You land on "Before we begin".
2. Try signing up again with the same email: it says an account already exists.
3. Tap Start the assessment and answer about 8 questions. Fully close the app (swipe it away) and reopen it: you come back to the assessment where you left off, not to the start.
4. Finish all 18 questions and the last "money why" question. After "Reading your patterns, not judging them..." you see your archetype with Strengths and Blind spots, plus two runner-up archetypes.
5. Read your archetype description: does it feel like you? Tell us honestly (spot on / partly / not at all) and why.
6. Tap Enter CUE: you're asked "Where does your money stand?". Enter a bank balance (cash is optional) and tap Continue. You land on Today.

Today
7. The greeting uses your first name, and Total balance matches what you entered.
8. Under Quick add, tap Cash, type "Swiggy dinner" and an amount: the category picks Food & delivery by itself. Tap Log: the spend appears under Today, and the bar for today in This week grows.
9. Log 3 or 4 more spends in different categories, including one with a long name: each one appears straight away with the right amount and category icon.
10. Tap a spend: its details open. Delete it: it disappears, and today's total and bar shrink.
11. Tap a different day in the week strip: it shows that day's spending (empty days show nothing, not an error). Tap today again.
12. Pull down on Today to refresh: everything reloads and nothing duplicates.

Balance and net worth
13. On Today, tap Update balance and change bank or cash: Total balance changes immediately.
14. Go to the You tab and tap the small balance chart: Net worth opens and shows your total net worth and a trend over the last 30 days.

Khata (lend / borrow)
15. Under Quick add, tap Lend / Borrow, choose I lent, enter a person's name, an amount and a note, and tap Save. It appears in the Khata tab under Money lent.
16. Add another entry with I borrowed and a different person: it appears under Money borrowed.
17. Add a second I lent entry for the same person as step 15: Khata shows that person once, with the two amounts added together.
18. Tap Pick from contacts: Android asks for contacts permission once, and picking a contact fills in the name. Saying "Deny" should not crash anything; you can still type the name.
19. Open a person in Khata and tap Mark settled on an entry: it disappears from the open list and their total updates.
20. Net worth (step 14) includes money lent to you and subtracts money you borrowed. Check it adds up.

Subscriptions
21. Under Quick add, tap Subscription and add e.g. "Netflix", ₹199, with billing day = tomorrow's date. It shows under Upcoming as "Due tomorrow".
22. Add a second subscription with the same name: it's refused with a clear message.
23. Log a spend through Cash with the category set to Subscription: you're asked for a billing day, and it also appears in your subscriptions.
24. Tomorrow at about 10 am, a notification asks "Is Netflix still active?". Tap it: CUE opens on Today and asks the same question. Tap Yes: the payment is logged as a spend for that day.
25. For another subscription, answer No when asked: it moves to Stopped and isn't asked about again.

Insights
26. Open the Insights tab after logging some spends: This week, Spend by day and Where it went (top categories) match what you logged.
27. On a brand-new account with no spends, Insights says "CUE will start noticing patterns as you use it." and shows no errors.

Nudges (these need a few days of normal use)
28. When you first log in, allow notifications when Android asks.
29. On the You tab, check your archetype under "The pattern CUE currently sees" and that the three Nudge preferences toggles (Pre-spend pause, Pattern nudges, Weekly reflection) switch on and off and stay that way after you close and reopen the app.
30. Log your real spends for 3 or more days. If you tend to order food around the same time, a nudge arrives about 15 minutes before your usual time. Tell us whether its timing and tone felt helpful or annoying.
31. After 3 or more spends on the same app (Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Uber, Amazon and so on) in a week, you may get a pattern nudge naming it.
32. On Sunday evening (around 6 pm), with some spends that week, you get a weekly reflection.
33. Turn Weekly reflection off: no Sunday nudge arrives. Turn it back on: it returns the next Sunday.
34. Note at most one spending nudge per day, and none late at night. Report any that break this.

Account and general
35. Fully close and reopen the app several times over a few days: you should stay signed in and never be asked to log in again.
36. Turn on airplane mode and open each tab: you see "Could not load. Pull to refresh." (not a crash or a blank screen). Turn airplane mode off and pull down: data comes back.
37. You tab > Retake assessment: go through it again. Your archetype updates afterwards and your spends and Khata are untouched.
38. Sign out from the You tab: you're back on the login screen and get no more nudges. Log back in with your email and password: all your data is still there.
39. If two of you share a phone: sign out, have the other person log in to their own account, and check none of the first person's spends, Khata or archetype show up.
40. Anything confusing, ugly, slow or missing that isn't covered above: write it down. First impressions matter most.
