CUE dashboard P1 test pass

This build adds money in and out to the Today tab: this month's income, spending, savings and dues; bills and subscriptions you can mark paid; a cash flow chart; a net worth chart; and a new look in your archetype's colours.

Install (Android only): open the APK link on your phone, tap Download, open the file, and allow "Install from this source" if Android asks. Install it over your current CUE: your account and data stay. Settings › Apps › CUE should say version 1.2.1.

Report each problem as: step number, what you did, what you saw, a screenshot or screen recording if possible.
Amounts can be made up. CUE only records what you type; no real money moves.

What is different from 1.2.0, so you're not surprised:
- Today has a new look and new fonts: navy text with your archetype's colour for accents, buttons and highlights. Sign-in and the assessment keep their old look for now.
- The weekly spending bars are gone. "Where your money moves" replaces them.
- Subscriptions no longer ask "Is it still active?" in a pop-up. Due payments wait in Coming up with a "Check" tag until you mark them paid or skip them.
- You can now pick days up to four weeks ahead on the date row. They show only what is due that day.

Already known, no need to report:
- Goal Jars are not in this build (they come next).
- "A note from CUE" still says "CUE is getting to know your patterns."
- The "this month" change under your net worth stays hidden until you have used CUE for a full previous month.
- Banks show a letter, not their logo.
- No iPhone version yet.
- The first open after a long quiet period can take up to a minute ("Waking CUE up").

Test the steps below one by one and write your result next to each (WORKS / DOESN'T / what happened).

The new look
1. Open Today. The background is a light tint of your archetype's colour, cards are white, amounts are in a serif font, and the + button is dark navy. Selected dates, links and chips are in your archetype's colour. Nothing overlaps or is cut off.
2. Scroll from top to bottom. The order is: greeting and dates, net worth, four round buttons, THIS MONTH tiles, A note from CUE, Your accounts, Where your money moves, Coming up, Today.

This month
3. Under THIS MONTH · [month] there are four tiles: Income, Expenses, Savings and Upcoming dues.
4. Log an income of ₹30,000 with category Salary, and another income of ₹5,000 with another category. The Income tile shows ₹35,000 and "Salary ₹30,000 · Other ₹5,000".
5. Log a few expenses. The Expenses tile shows the total and "₹… a day on average". Check: total ÷ today's date number = the daily average.
6. The Savings tile shows income − expenses and "…% of income saved".
7. New account (or before any income this month): the Income tile says "Add income". Tapping it opens the income form. The Savings tile says "Add income to see your savings rate".
8. If you spent more than came in, Savings shows "−₹…" in normal colours, never red.
9. Tap Income: total, Salary, Other income, Recurring income, six monthly bars and this month's income list.
10. Tap Expenses: the total, the daily average, "↑ or ↓ ₹… vs same days last month", spending by category (largest first) and the list. Tap a category: Transactions opens showing only that category.
11. Tap Savings: income, expenses, savings, savings rate and the last six months.

Salary and recurring income
12. On the Income screen tap "Set up salary". Enter Salary, an amount, Monthly, the next payday and the account. Save. It shows under Recurring income with "Next [date]".
13. Back on Today, under your net worth a line says "₹… free to spend today". Check it roughly: (bank + cash − bills due before payday) ÷ days until payday.
14. Log an income, open More options, set Recurring to Monthly. The next date fills in a month later. Save. It also appears under Recurring income.
15. In Transactions, pick the Recurring filter: only entries saved as recurring or marked paid show.

Coming up
16. Tap the + button, then "Subscription or bill". Add Netflix, ₹199, Monthly, due in two days, from your bank. It shows in Coming up with "HDFC · Monthly · 🔔" (your account's name) and a pill "In 2 days".
17. Add a bill due today (pill "Today") and one due in more than 3 days (pill shows the date, like "25 Nov").
18. Add a rent of ₹12,000 with a due date in the past. It shows on top with a "Check" pill. It is not counted as spent.
19. Swipe a Coming up row to the left: Mark paid and Skip appear.
20. Mark paid: a short sheet shows the amount, account and date (Today). Change the amount for a bill that varies and confirm. A message says "… marked paid · Undo". The balance of that account goes down and the item leaves Coming up.
21. Tap Undo straight away: the payment disappears and the item is back.
22. Skip one: it leaves Coming up; "Skipped … · Undo" brings it back. Skipping never spends money.
23. Tap a row: its details open with Mark paid, Skip this one, Edit and Cancel. Edit the amount and save. Cancel it: it disappears from Coming up; past payments stay in Transactions.
24. Tap View calendar: a month grid with dots on days with payments and "₹… due this month" at the top. Tap a day with a dot: its payments show below. Paid and skipped ones look greyed. Use the arrows to see next month.
25. Tap the Upcoming dues tile: dues grouped by Subscriptions, Bills, Rent, EMIs, each with Mark paid.
26. Pick a day next week on the date row (use › to move a week ahead). Coming up changes to "DUE [date]" and shows only what is due that day. The Today list is hidden for future days.
27. With a future day picked, tap + and log an expense: the button says Schedule. It appears in Coming up on that day, not in your transactions, and your balance does not change.
28. Add a payment due tomorrow with the reminder on (the default). Tomorrow at 10 am your phone shows "[name] is due today". Tapping it opens Today.

Where your money moves
29. The card has Week, Month and 3 Months. Week shows seven pairs of bars, Monday to Sunday: green for money in, blue for money out, and a teal line for what's left.
30. Transfers between your own accounts do not change these bars.
31. Tap a bar: a strip says "[day] · Spent ₹… · [n] transactions · View". Tap View: Transactions opens for that day.
32. Month shows the weeks of this month; 3 Months shows three months. Received, Spent and Remaining under the chart match the bars.
33. Pick a past day on the date row: that day is highlighted on the chart.
34. A brand-new account with under 3 days of data shows "Your money's movement shows up here after a few days of logging."

Net worth chart
35. On the net worth card a small line shows the last 30 days. Tap it: the Net worth screen opens on 30D.
36. The chart has 7D, 30D, 3M, 6M and 1Y. Drag your finger along the line: the date and net worth show above it.
37. Lift your finger on a day: "Your position on [date]" opens with that day's assets, liabilities and net worth.
38. Pick 1Y with less than a year of data: the line starts at your first balance and a note says "History starts [date]".

Everywhere
39. Tap the eye icon: every amount, including tiles, charts, Coming up and the new screens, shows ₹ •••••. Percentages stay visible.
40. Airplane mode, pull down on Today: the new cards keep their figures. Reopen offline: each new card says "Couldn't load…" with Try again on its own.
