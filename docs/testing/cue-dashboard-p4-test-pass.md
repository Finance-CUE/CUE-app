CUE dashboard P4 test pass

This build makes CUE notice things for you: a note on Today about what deserves your attention, Ask CUE for quick questions and quick logging, and categories that learn from your corrections.

Install (Android only): open the APK link on your phone, tap Download, open the file, and allow "Install from this source" if Android asks. Install it over your current CUE: your account and data stay. Settings › Apps › CUE should say version 1.2.4.

Report each problem as: step number, what you did, what you saw, a screenshot or screen recording if possible.
Amounts can be made up. CUE only records what you type; no real money moves.

How it works, so you're not surprised:
- Notes come only from your own entries. CUE notices; it never judges ("is up", never "you overspent").
- One note at a time. The same note doesn't come back for 7 days, and × shows the next one.
- Ask CUE reads what you type with simple rules, not AI. It answers from your entries, or fills a form for you to check. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Already known, no need to report:
- Ask CUE understands common questions and logging sentences; unusual phrasing gets examples instead of an answer.
- No voice entry or receipts yet.
- Everything listed as known in the 1.2.3 guide still applies.

Test the steps below one by one and write your result next to each (WORKS / DOESN'T / what happened).

A note from CUE
1. On a new account (under a week of entries), "A note from CUE" says "CUE is getting to know your patterns."
2. With a payment due in the next 3 days, the note says it is due (for example "Netflix (₹199) is due on 18 Nov.").
3. Tap Understand this: a sheet shows the numbers behind the note and the entries that drove it.
4. Tap × on the note: it goes away and the next note (if any) shows. Close and reopen the app: the dismissed note doesn't come back.
5. Lend someone money more than 14 days ago (use Another day): the note says "₹… pending from [name]." once nothing more urgent is due.
6. Spend clearly more on one category this month than on the same days last month: the note says "Your [category] spending is up …% on the same days last month." Understand this lists the biggest spends.
7. A Goal Jar at 25%, 50% or 75%: the note says "[jar] jar is 50% complete."
8. View insights opens the matching screen (Upcoming dues, Khata, Expenses or the jar).
9. For an archetype with a result, Understand this on a spending note shows a short line about your archetype.

Ask CUE
10. On Today, tap the sparkle ✦ next to your initials: Ask CUE opens with example questions.
11. Ask "How much did I spend on food this month?": it answers with the amount.
12. Try: "Who owes me money?", "What's due this week?", "What's my net worth?", "How much is in cash?", "How much did I save this month?", "What was my biggest spend this month?", "Compare food with last month".
13. Type "I spent ₹500 on dinner yesterday using HDFC". CUE says "Expense ₹500 · Food & delivery, yesterday from HDFC". Tap Check and save: the expense form opens filled in, dated yesterday. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
14. Try "Moved ₹5,000 from HDFC to cash", "lent 2000 to Ravi", "received salary 30000": each opens the right form filled in.
15. Type something CUE can't read (for example "tell me a joke"): it says so and offers examples.
16. Tap + then the Ask CUE chip under the tiles: Ask CUE opens.
17. On the note, tap Ask CUE: it opens with a question about that note already answered.
18. Tap the eye icon, then ask a question: the answer shows ₹ ••••• instead of amounts.

Categories that learn
19. Long-press any expense in Today's list or Transactions: Change category opens.
20. Pick a different category. CUE asks "Always put [merchant] in [category]?". Tap Always.
21. Add a new expense with the same first word (for example "Zepto order" after fixing a Zepto entry): the remembered category is picked for you.
22. Tap "Just this one" on another change: only that entry changes.
23. Long-press a transfer or a group expense: nothing opens (they have no category to change here).

Colours
24. Retake the assessment and get a different archetype: going back to Today, the new colours fade in smoothly.
