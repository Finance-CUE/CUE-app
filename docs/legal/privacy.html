Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

CUE ("we", "us") is an app that helps you understand your money habits. This policy explains what information CUE collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, write to us at support@cuemoney.in.

The short version

  • We collect only what CUE needs to work: your account details, your answers to the money assessment, and the money entries you type in.
  • CUE does not connect to your bank, read your SMS, or see your card details. Everything in CUE is what you enter yourself.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

What we collect

Account details. Your name, email address, mobile number and password. Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google profile picture link from Google. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

Assessment answers. Your answers to the CUE money assessment and the money profile (archetype) we calculate from them.

Money entries. What you choose to record in CUE, such as spends, balances, starting money, subscriptions, assets, and Khata entries (money you lent or borrowed), along with any notes you add.

Contacts (only if you allow it). When you add a Khata entry you can pick a person from your phone's contacts. CUE reads only the name of the one contact you pick, and saves that name with the entry. We do not upload your contact list or anyone's phone number.

Technical information. Like most online services, our servers record basic request information, such as IP address, time of request and error details, to keep the service secure, stop abuse and fix problems. These logs are kept for a short period.

Notifications. CUE's reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to keep you signed in on your devices.
  • To send you verification and password reset codes by email.
  • To show you your money profile, your entries and the insights built from them.
  • To keep CUE safe, for example by limiting repeated failed logins.
  • To understand and fix problems, and to improve CUE.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run CUE. They handle data only on our instructions and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and our database.
  • Render: hosting for CUE's servers.
  • Resend: sending verification and password reset emails.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".

Some of these providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of CUE's users.

How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your entries are linked to your account and can only be read through it; other users cannot see them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something for longer.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete your account and data;
  • withdraw consent, for example by turning off contacts access or notifications;
  • raise a complaint with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To do any of this, email support@cuemoney.in from the address linked to your account. We reply within 30 days.

Children

CUE is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CUE changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top, and if the change is significant we will let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: support@cuemoney.in
